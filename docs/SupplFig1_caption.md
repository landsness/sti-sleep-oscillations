# Supplementary Figure 1 — caption

**Supplementary Figure 1. Photothrombotic infarcts are concentrated in superficial-to-mid cortical layers and spare the deep cortex in both STI groups.**

Group coronal lesion-incidence maps in a normalized cortical reference frame, shown separately for animals with (STI+, left; *N* = 14) and without (STI−, right; *N* = 11) secondary thalamic injury. For each animal, the infarct outline, pia, gray/white-matter boundary, and midline were traced (ImageJ) on the coronal section of maximal infarct cross-sectional area, and each infarct was warped into a common cortical frame. The vertical axis is **cortical depth**, normalized so that 0 is the pia and 1 the gray/white-matter boundary (dashed line). The horizontal axis is mediolateral distance from the midline expressed in **cortical-thickness units** — multiples of the local pia-to-white-matter thickness at that mediolateral position; because both axes are scaled to cortical thickness, the map is independent of section magnification and is displayed at equal aspect so cortical proportions are preserved. Each animal's warped infarct was rasterized to a binary mask and averaged within group; color indicates the percentage of animals in the group with infarct present at each location (see color bar).
